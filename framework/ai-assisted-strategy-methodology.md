# AI-Assisted Strategy Methodology

## Principle
AI accelerates research and synthesis. Management owns the decision.

## Workflow
1. Frame the business problem and management question.
2. Build a human hypothesis from operating experience.
3. Use AI to broaden research, structure comparisons and expose alternatives.
4. Validate material claims against source evidence and business data.
5. Translate evidence into strategic choices, economics, risks and priorities.
6. Apply commercial judgment and management calibration.
7. Convert the decision into pilots, KPIs, roadmaps and executive deliverables.

AI is not presented as the author of the strategy. It is a tool inside a management process.
