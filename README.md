## What this portfolio demonstrates

**Growth strategy** — diagnosing the mechanism behind a commercial problem rather than treating symptoms.

**International expansion** — market prioritization, entry sequencing, partnerships and channel architecture.

**Marketplace monetization** — pricing, packaging, data products, media, ancillary revenue and transaction layers.

**Product & commercial alignment** — translating revenue objectives into product choices, pilots and measurable KPIs.

**AI-assisted strategy** — using generative AI for research acceleration, synthesis, comparison and rapid prototyping while preserving source validation and human management judgment.

**Executive communication** — turning analysis into clear, interactive management deliverables.

## Working method

`Human experience & hypothesis → AI-assisted research and synthesis → source validation → strategic analysis → commercial judgment → executable plan`

## Confidentiality & sanitization

These are **portfolio reconstructions based on real consulting work**. They are not the original client proposals.

Client identities, executive names and contact information, passwords, commercial terms, compensation, target-account lists, relationship warmth, confidential pipeline information, proprietary operating details and selected figures have been removed, generalized or intentionally modified.

The strategic frameworks, problem-solving approach and management logic are preserved.
