# Closing the Listings Gap

**Sanitized portfolio case · AI-Assisted Growth Strategy Casebook**

## Strategic challenge
Commercial-model redesign for an automotive marketplace where listing-slot economics were creating incentives that conflicted with inventory breadth.

## Core thesis
When dealers rationally load more stock onto a competitor, the problem may be the incentive architecture — not the effort of the sales team.

## Portfolio disclosure
This public version is derived from real advisory work. Client identity, executive contacts, target accounts, commercial terms, relationship information and selected operating figures have been removed, generalized or intentionally modified. The strategic methodology and management logic are preserved.
