# From Leads to Trusted Transactions

**Sanitized portfolio case · AI-Assisted Growth Strategy Casebook**

## Strategic challenge
International expansion strategy for a transaction-protection layer designed to move classifieds and vertical marketplaces beyond lead generation.

## Core thesis
The opportunity sits where marketplaces generate intent but deliberately stop short of handling money — leaving a trust and attribution gap between lead and transaction.

## Portfolio disclosure

Client identity, executive contacts, target accounts, commercial terms, relationship information and selected operating figures have been removed, generalized or intentionally modified. The strategic methodology and management logic are preserved.
