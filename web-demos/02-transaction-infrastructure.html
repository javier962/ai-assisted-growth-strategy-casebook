02 · Strategic choices

Separate fast tests from structural moves

Recommendations are organized as a portfolio: actions that can generate evidence quickly sit alongside deeper changes that create durable advantage.

Lead wedgeAutomotive reservation

Clear intent signal, attributable dealer value and a compact transaction flow.

Second waveProperty deposit

Strong consumer pain, but local legal and escrow conventions require sequencing.

PriorityOpen marketplace ecosystems

Start where portals are open to transaction infrastructure and localization friction is manageable.

Selective expansionFintech-heavy markets

Avoid head-on competition where integrated payment ecosystems already dominate.

05 · Management insight

The strategic product is not simply escrow — it is a bridge from lead generation to attributable transaction intent.

A marketplace can deepen consumer trust and prove more value to professional customers without necessarily becoming the balance-sheet owner of the transaction.

06 · AI-assisted working method

AI accelerates research and synthesis — it does not replace judgment.

Human hypothesis → AI-assisted research → source validation → strategic analysis → commercial judgment → executable plan

AI is used to widen research, structure comparisons, stress-test alternatives and accelerate deliverable creation. Recommendations remain grounded in source evidence, operating context and management judgment.
