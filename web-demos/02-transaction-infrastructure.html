SANITIZED CASE 02

From Leads to Trusted Transactions

International expansion strategy for a transaction-protection layer designed to move marketplaces beyond lead generation.

01 · Diagnosis

Find the mechanism behind the symptom

The work starts by identifying the commercial, product or market mechanism producing the observed outcome before prescribing technology, sales activity or expansion.

Business signal

Separate visible symptoms from the structural cause.

Market evidence

Use competitive and ecosystem evidence to test the initial hypothesis.

Management question

Translate analysis into a decision the leadership team can act on.

02 · Strategic choices

Separate fast tests from structural moves

Recommendations are organized as a portfolio: actions that can generate evidence quickly sit alongside deeper changes that create durable advantage.

Quick wins

Low-friction actions that generate evidence and momentum.

Supporting layer

Capabilities that improve distribution, discovery or conversion.

Major bets

Structural changes with greater strategic upside and execution requirements.

New revenue

Commercial models that convert differentiated capability into recurring value.

03 · Execution architecture

Turn strategy into a measurable sequence

The strategy is translated into prioritized actions, owners, pilots, evidence thresholds and management review points rather than ending with a presentation.

Validate

Confirm assumptions and define the smallest credible test.

Pilot

Run controlled market, product or partnership experiments.

Measure & decide

Scale, adapt or stop based on evidence and strategic fit.
