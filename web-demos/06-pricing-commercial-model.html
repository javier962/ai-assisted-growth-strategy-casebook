SANITIZED PORTFOLIO CASE · 06

Closing the Listings Gap

Commercial-model redesign for an automotive marketplace where listing-slot economics conflicted with inventory breadth.

If the package prices scarcity, dealers rationally keep their full stock elsewhere. The commercial model must reward breadth and monetize performance on top.
Breadthcore mechanic
Volumepricing basis
Boostsvalue layer

01 · Diagnosis

A capped model can create the inventory gap it is trying to monetize.

When a dealer holds far more vehicles than its paid slot allowance, the rational behavior is to keep full inventory on the uncapped competitor and use the capped marketplace as a curated shop window. That weakens consumer choice and network effects.

Dealer incentive

The package architecture determines where the full stock is uploaded.

Marketplace effect

Less breadth weakens search utility and the consumer proposition.

Commercial paradox

Scarcity may protect short-term package yield while eroding the inventory base that creates long-term value.

02 · Global playbook

Price the stock base; monetize visibility above it.

The review compared volume-based, average-active-stock and uncapped models. Across these structures, the common lesson is to make breadth easy and charge for differentiated performance or visibility on top.

Volume bands

Price according to the dealer’s active stock with improving effective unit economics at scale.

Average active stock

Tie subscription tiers to real inventory rather than a rigid slot ceiling.

Uncapped option

Use a predictable flat fee where strategic inventory growth matters more than per-unit scarcity.

Premium layer

Featured placement, performance products and ancillary services sit above the core access model.

03 · Design principles

Separate access from acceleration.

The redesign should preserve a broad core marketplace while creating a clear ladder of paid value.

Core access

Make it economically rational to load the full book.

Visibility

Charge for prominence, not permission to exist.

Performance

Add products tied to leads, conversion or measurable outcomes.

Ancillary value

Finance, valuation, wholesale or dealer tools can deepen ARPA without restricting supply.

04 · Transition architecture

A pricing redesign is also a migration problem.

Changing the mechanic affects existing contracts, dealer expectations and revenue timing. The transition therefore needs cohort analysis, grandfathering rules, pilot packages and explicit revenue guardrails.

Model cohorts

Simulate impact by dealer size and current package.

Pilot

Test with a representative dealer group before broad migration.

Protect revenue

Define floors, migration incentives and premium-product upsell.

Measure inventory

Track full-stock adoption as a leading strategic KPI.

05 · Management dashboard

Measure whether the new mechanic changes behavior.

The redesigned model should be judged on marketplace health as well as immediate subscription revenue.

Inventory breadth

Share of dealer stock represented on-platform.

Dealer penetration

Active paying dealers and package migration.

Lead efficiency

Leads per active vehicle and dealer.

Premium attach

Adoption of featured/performance products.

Revenue quality

ARPA and retention after migration.
