05 · Management insight

Data becomes commercially valuable when it changes a customer decision.

The strongest monetization products help customers sell, buy, price or allocate spend better — rather than simply charging for access to inventory.

06 · AI-assisted working method

AI accelerates research and synthesis — it does not replace judgment.

Human hypothesis → AI-assisted research → source validation → strategic analysis → commercial judgment → executable plan

AI is used to widen research, structure comparisons, stress-test alternatives and accelerate deliverable creation. Recommendations remain grounded in source evidence, operating context and management judgment.
