01 · Market prioritization

LATAM is a portfolio, not a single market

The decision framework separates addressable demand from practical access, partner leverage, localization and economics.

Expansion moves from validation to pilots, comparative evidence and selective scale.

05 · Management takeaway

LATAM is neither one market nor a collection of unrelated countries. Reuse what travels; localize what changes the economics.

Client identity, target accounts, personal relationships, commercial terms and selected operating figures have been removed, generalized or intentionally modified. The strategic methodology and decision logic are preserved.
