SANITIZED PORTFOLIO CASE · 05

Rebuilding an Automotive Marketplace Growth Engine

A competitive diagnosis and prioritized growth plan spanning consumer UX, organic discovery, dealer value and new B2B monetization.

Protect marketplace leadership by fixing consumer friction, strengthening trust and discovery, and converting operational marketing capability into dealer value.
3priority layers
UXfirst
SEO/GEOresilience
B2Bnew revenue
01 · Competitive diagnosis

The specialist portal can lead its category while still leaking demand.

The original review found the most important threat was not another specialist classified site but a frictionless social marketplace with free publishing, native messaging and social trust signals. At the same time, heavy organic-search dependence created exposure as AI changes discovery.

Consumer threat

Zero-friction publishing and messaging reset user expectations.

Discovery risk

Dependence on organic search requires long-tail content and structured inventory.

Trust gap

Verified sellers and vehicle-history signals can strengthen the specialist proposition.

02 · Quick wins

Fix the browsing and inventory experience first.

The action plan separates high-impact, lower-effort product improvements from longer structural bets.

Result cards

Improve information density and make the value proposition visible before the click.

Mobile browsing

Reduce friction with continuous discovery patterns.

Structured vehicle data

Make technical fields consistent enough to power filters, SEO and AI discovery.

Inventory quality

Detect duplicate and low-quality bulk content before it degrades search.

03 · Supporting layer

Build discovery resilience beyond the listing page.

Editorial content, long-tail SEO and cleaner structured data expand the number of useful entry points while improving the material available to search engines and AI assistants.

Long-tail content

Guides and comparisons around real purchase questions.

Saved-search communication

Persistent messaging and alerts bring demand back.

Paid acquisition

Diversify traffic rather than relying on one discovery source.

04 · Major bets

Deepen trust and retention.

Higher-effort initiatives create reasons for consumers to remain inside the marketplace rather than jumping to a social channel.

Persistent chat

Keep the conversation and history attached to the marketplace journey.

Verified seller

Add explicit trust signals for private and professional sellers.

Vehicle history

Bring decision-support information closer to the listing.

05 · New B2B revenue

Turn marketing capability into a dealer product.

The strategy also identifies an adjacent managed-media proposition: use structured dealer inventory to create automated video and paid-social campaigns, transforming an internal capability into a recurring B2B service.

Inventory → creative

Generate campaign assets from live stock.

Managed distribution

Run off-platform campaigns against dealer objectives.

Measurement

Tie spend to attributable dealer demand and renewal logic.
