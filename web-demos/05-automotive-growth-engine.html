01 · Competitive diagnosis

The threat is frictionless discovery

A specialist marketplace can lead its category while still leaking demand to social marketplaces and new discovery layers.

THREATFrictionless alternatives

Free publishing, native messaging and social trust reset expectations.

PRODUCTEngagement redesign

Improve result cards, mobile browsing, saved-search communication and structured data.

REVENUEDealer media

Turn inventory and campaign capability into a recurring B2B service.

The plan combines immediate UX and discovery fixes with deeper trust and monetization bets.

Client identity, target accounts, personal relationships, commercial terms and selected operating figures have been removed, generalized or intentionally modified. The strategic methodology and decision logic are preserved.
